From download to working together.

01

Make a team space

Sign in with Google and create a project. Share an invitation key with someone you want to collaborate with.

02

Connect your folder

Open the desktop app, sign in through this website, select the project and choose a folder on your computer.

03

Work with your agents

Reload your coding sessions and approve the project integration. Agents can check team activity and reserve files before publishing changes.

Shared publishing rejects conflicting changes. Ordinary editor writes cannot be blocked by COORD; divergent local files are preserved for review. Account projects sync over HTTPS and remain available when the creator’s computer goes offline.
